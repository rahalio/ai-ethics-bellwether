# Bellwether — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Board technology and ethics committee chair

- As committee chair, I want each material technology decision to arrive with its unnecessary harms named and either mitigated or explicitly accepted, so that the committee is approving a trade-off rather than a business case.
- As committee chair, I want to see where our stated position on consent or displacement trails what regulators are converging on, so that I know whether we are leading, keeping pace or exposed.
- As committee chair, I want every short-term sacrifice we accepted brought back to us at its horizon date with evidence on whether the long-term benefit appeared, so that ethical decisions are judged on outcomes rather than intentions.
- As committee chair, I want a between-meetings decision to be possible under delegated authority with mandatory ratification at the next meeting, so that the committee is not routed around because the calendar did not fit.

### Chief corporate affairs officer (radar operator)

- As the operator, I want exposure tracked continuously across the five domains for each stakeholder group, so that I can brief the committee on movement rather than reassembling a picture each quarter.
- As the operator, I want engagements to record what we heard and what we changed, so that I can demonstrate that consultation altered a decision rather than decorated it.
- As the operator, I want an alert when a decision proceeds without a harm assessment, so that a threshold breach is escalated while it can still be corrected.

### Business unit leader

- As a business unit leader, I want to know at the earliest stage which harms my decision will be assessed against, so that I can design them out rather than defend them at committee.
- As a business unit leader, I want to argue that a harm is necessary — inseparable from the benefit — and have that argument recorded and adjudicated, so that the assessment is a judgement rather than a veto.
- As a business unit leader, I want to record that I chose incremental adoption and why, so that a slower rollout reads as a deliberate governance choice rather than as delivery weakness.

### HR transition lead and employee representative

- As an HR transition lead, I want the roles affected, the retraining provision and the consultation evidence attached to the decision itself, so that displacement is governed with the decision that causes it rather than afterwards.
- As an employee representative, I want to record the workforce's position before board approval and to see whether the decision changed as a result, so that consultation has a visible consequence.
- As an employee representative, I want to report that a decision is being pushed through despite unresolved workforce harm, and to have that report reach the committee without passing my own management chain. *(exception path)*

### Company secretary and internal audit

- As the Company Secretary, I want the harm assessment, the alternatives considered, the position taken and the dissent recorded as one immutable board record, so that a decision taken under today's understanding can be explained years later under a different one. *(governance / admin story)*
- As an internal auditor, I want every unnecessary harm accepted without mitigation listed with its accepting executive and any expiry, so that acceptances age visibly rather than becoming the company's de facto standard.
- As the Company Secretary, I want conscience-channel reports tracked to a recorded outcome with reporter protection evidenced, so that the channel's credibility is measurable rather than asserted.
